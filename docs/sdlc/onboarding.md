# Onboarding

This guide helps new team members understand Field Techy's SDLC processes and tools.

## Day 1: Orientation
- Review [Scrum Framework](scrum-framework-overview.md), [Roles](roles.md), and [Artifacts](artifacts.md).
- Set up environment: Git, IDE, access to repos.

## Week 1: Processes
- Attend Sprint Planning and Daily Scrums.
- Learn [Git Flow](git-flow.md): Branching from `dev`, reviews, merges to `qa` and `main`.
- Study [QA Processes](qa-processes.md) and [DoD](artifacts.md).

## Tools Training
- GitHub: Issues, PRs.
- CI/CD: Pipeline basics.
- Testing: Unit and integration.

## Resources
- This SDLC doc.
- Team wiki for Field Techy specifics.

Contact Scrum Master for questions. Welcome aboard!
