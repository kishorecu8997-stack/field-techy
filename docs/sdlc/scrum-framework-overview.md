# Scrum Framework Overview

The Scrum framework is an agile methodology used for managing complex product development, emphasizing iterative progress, collaboration, and flexibility. For the Field Techy platform, Scrum guides the entire development team in delivering high-quality features across the web application, backend services, and related modules through time-boxed sprints.

## Core Principles
Scrum is founded on empiricism, where decisions are based on observation and experimentation rather than rigid planning. Key principles include:
- **Transparency:** All aspects of the process must be visible to stakeholders.
- **Inspection:** Regular checks on progress and artifacts.
- **Adaptation:** Adjustments based on feedback and changing requirements.

## Values
Scrum promotes five core values to foster a productive environment:
- **Commitment:** Team members commit to goals and support each other.
- **Courage:** Willingness to tackle challenges and admit mistakes.
- **Focus:** Concentration on sprint goals without distractions.
- **Openness:** Honest communication about progress and issues.
- **Respect:** Valuing each other's skills and contributions.

## Application to Field Techy
In Field Techy, Scrum is applied to entire development (e.g., APIs for job posting by a client, UI for engineer sign up) and web modules. Sprints typically last 2 weeks, allowing rapid iteration on features like client notifications or engineer profiles. The framework ensures alignment with business needs, such as quick responses to market demands in the field services industry.

## Benefits
- Faster delivery of increments.
- Improved quality through continuous testing and feedback.
- Enhanced team collaboration and adaptability to changes.
