# Escalation of Issues

This document outlines the process for escalating issues, including blockers and performance concerns, to ensure timely resolution and team accountability in Field Techy.

## Escalation for Issues/Blockers
- **Immediate Communication:** Post under the user story with the specified format (To: @product_owner, CC: @team_members, description). See [Team Communication](communication.md).
- **Formal Channels:** Inform via WhatsApp group and mention in the next sprint meeting.
- **PO Involvement:** If unresolved after 24 hours, escalate directly to [PO](roles.md) for prioritization or resource allocation.
- **Timeline:** Aim for resolution within the sprint; carry over to backlog if needed.

## Escalation for Non-Performing Team Members
- **Identification:** During [Sprint Retrospective](events.md), flag non-performing members using KPT feedback.
- **Private Discussion:** [Scrum Master](roles.md) conducts one-on-one coaching sessions post-retrospective.
- **Formal Escalation:** If no improvement, escalate to [PO](roles.md) for performance review or reassignment.
- **Documentation:** Record in team notes; involve HR if necessary for Field Techy policies.
- **Support:** Provide training or resources before escalation to foster improvement.

## General Guidelines
- Escalations should be constructive and data-driven.
- Maintain confidentiality for performance issues.
- Track escalations in GitHub issues for transparency.

This process promotes accountability and continuous improvement.
