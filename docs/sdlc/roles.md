# Roles

Scrum defines clear roles to ensure accountability and efficient collaboration. In the Field Techy project, these roles guide the development of features across all the application components, including web and backend components.

## Product Owner (PO)

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **Responsibilities** | Defines and prioritizes the Product Backlog based on stakeholder needs. Communicates vision and requirements for features like job postings or user dashboards. Accepts or rejects work results during Sprint Reviews. Collaborates with the team to refine user stories and ensure business value. |
| **Key Interactions** | Works closely with stakeholders and the Scrum Master to remove impediments. |

## Scrum Master (SM)

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **Responsibilities** | Facilitates Scrum events and ensures adherence to the framework. Coaches the team on Scrum practices and removes obstacles. Protects the team from external distractions during sprints. Promotes continuous improvement through retrospectives. |
| **Key Interactions** | Acts as a servant-leader, supporting the Product Owner and Development Team. |

## Development Team

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **Composition** | Cross-functional group including backend developers, frontend developers, QA engineers, and designers (typically 5-9 members). |
| **Responsibilities** | Self-organizes to deliver potentially releasable increments each sprint. Handles tasks like API development, UI implementation, and testing for Field Techy modules. Participates in all Scrum events and commits to sprint goals. Enforces the [Definition of Done (DoD)](artifacts.md) for quality assurance. |
| **Key Principle** | No sub-teams; everyone contributes to all aspects, including QA integration. |
| **QA Integration** | QA engineers are part of the team, ensuring testing is embedded in development. QA ensures PRs are reviewed/approved/merged within timelines and escalates delays to PO. See [QA Processes](qa-processes.md) and [Escalation](escalation.md). |

## Role Interactions
- PO provides direction; SM facilitates; Team executes.
- All roles collaborate in [events](events.md) like Daily Scrum and Retrospectives.
- In Field Techy, this structure supports rapid iteration on features such as job discovery by engineer or client posting a job on the portal.

These roles ensure balanced input and prevent bottlenecks in the SDLC.
